Company Travel Policy

Employees traveling for approved business purposes can claim
reimbursement for eligible travel expenses.

Domestic travel:
Employees can claim up to $300 per night for hotel accommodation.

International travel:
Employees can claim up to $500 per night for hotel accommodation.

Employees must submit receipts within 30 days of completing their trip.

Business class flights are allowed only for flights longer than
8 hours.

Personal travel expenses are not reimbursable.

---

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.

Employees must receive manager approval before working remotely.

Remote employees are responsible for maintaining a suitable
workspace.

The company provides a $500 annual allowance for approved
home-office equipment.

---

Employee Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave.

Employees become eligible for paid annual leave after completing
3 months of employment.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward for up to 10 days.

Employees must request leave at least 3 working days in advance.

___

Business Expense Policy

Employees traveling for work can claim reimbursement for
reasonable business-related expenses.

Accommodation expenses must comply with the applicable
travel limits.

Employees should use the standard corporate travel portal
when booking accommodation.

Luxury accommodation is not reimbursable unless explicitly
approved by a department head.


Travel Approval Policy

International business trips require approval from the
employee's manager before the trip begins.

Trips lasting more than 14 days require additional approval
from the department head.

Employees should submit international travel requests at
least 10 business days before departure.
